A life story, in your own words

Mum, Dad —

We know your stories — or at least, we think we do. We know the jobs, the places, the rough order of things. But if I'm honest, we know the headlines more than the detail. The small bits that make a moment real. What it actually felt like at the time.

Your childhoods feel further away to us than perhaps they do to you. The stories of Grandma and Grandpa, of Nana and Grandad — what they were really like, what it was like growing up around them — are the kind of thing that fades quietly if nobody ever writes it down. And your own experience of being parents — the joys, the hard bits, what was going on in your lives when each of us arrived — is something we've never properly understood from your side.

That's what this is for. Ten chapters, one life stage at a time — a chance to tell it properly, in your own words, with all the detail that usually gets left out. There's no rush. You can do a session, close the page, and pick it up whenever suits you.

The questions will feel like me, because in a sense they are.

This is for all of us — James, Robert, Margaret and me, and whoever comes next.

Just start talking. I'll do the rest.
